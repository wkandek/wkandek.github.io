# Anthropic Mythos Preview Capabilities

Are they real, or can Opus, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Qwen 3.5 do the same thing?  
Anthropic has been alarmist before.  

This time it looks real.

Quick context: Mythos Preview (MP) is Anthropic's unreleased cyber-capability model. Project Glasswing is the security oriented consortium with 40+ members that have acces sto MP.

To check let's look at Google, a prominent Glasswing member. Why would they agree to participate?  

They have a frontier lab themselves, compete with Anthropic. But they also have a 10%+ stake n Anthropic through investments and sell compute to Anthropic.

Nicholas Carlini, the technical face of Anthropic's cybersecurity story, used to work at Google.

The following is speculation. No insider knowledge.

Carlini takes MP and points it at open source, including Chromium and Android. He's used Opus on both codebases before and it was uneventful. Both are well audited. Google has "Big Sleep" — their DeepMind-built vulnerability-finding agent — that has been checking for and fixing vulnerabilities for years.  

MP finds vulnerabilities. It narrows them down to exploitable ones. It writes exploits. That is surprising to Carlini.

Carlini checks with his contacts at Google. They pass him to the security team, one of the best in the world.  

They look at the samples. Initially they don't believe it, but they're pros. Remember: they run their own AI against the code, and frequently double-check with Opus and ChatGPT. Their code get evaluated by independent security researchers do the same. There are competitions for finding vulnerabilities out there with thousands of dollars payouts. Google itself paid $250,000 for a sandbox escape in 2025. 

They find the vulnerabilities are real.

They check back with Carlini: 

Google: How big was the team, how long did it work?  
Carlini: Myself and an intern and MP, one week.
